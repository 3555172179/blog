Picked up an ice-maker client last month. Their organic traffic was stuck at 200 UV/day. SEO tools showed solid keyword rankings, but orders just weren't coming.

.

Found a common but overlooked problem: **the right keywords, wrong search intent**. Someone searching "ice maker" on Alibaba and someone searching "ice maker for bar" have completely different buying intent.

## Method 1: Reverse-engineer from closed orders

Open "My Alibaba → Data Bank → Orders", → download the last 30 days of closed orders. Pull every inquiry title into a spreadsheet, run a word-frequency count.

You'll see a magical pattern: **the words that close are not the words that drive traffic**. Traffic is "ice maker", but the actual closers are "ice maker for bar", "ice maker countertop", "ice maker with ice scoop". Lower search volume, lower competition, way higher conversion.

Push those into the first 30 characters of product titles (Alibaba's algorithm weights that position highest). Organic traffic jumped 60% in one week for this client.

## Method 2: The "deleted keywords" tab in P4P

P4P backend has a buried tab tab: "Search Term Report → Deleted Keywords". It shows keywords you once bid on but the system killed.

These aren't worthless — the algorithm killed them as "low CTR, high spend". If you spot a long-tail like "ice maker with ice scoop", it means real search volume exists but your bid was wrong. Put it back in natural search titles. Free traffic.

## Method 3: RFQ buyer profiles

Open any RFQ inquiry detail. Look at the buyer's "Industry", "Purchase Frequency", "Purchase Quantity". Aggregate these across inquiries. Find buyers whose frequency is "monthly" and quantity is "container-load" → check which countries they come from → those country-language variants of your search terms are your blind spot.

For this client, RFQs showed heavy buyers from South Africa, Middle East, South America. None of these regions search primarily in English. Translating product detail pages into Spanish and Arabic lifted natural search another 30%.

## The takeaway

SEO isn't about stuffing keywords. It's about **matching the right keyword to the right person**. Orders tell you intent. Keyword tools tell you volume. Trust orders first.
