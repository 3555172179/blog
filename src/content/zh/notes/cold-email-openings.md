做了 2 年 B2B 外贸，客户开发信发了上千封。开头前 3 句决定了对方是否往下读——所以这 3 句必须「看起来不像模板」。

## 5 个万能开头

### 1. 痛点切入（适合 ToB 工具类）
> I noticed your team has been posting [X] jobs for [specific role] — managing [X] at [scale] usually doesn't work with [existing tool]; it's a sign your current workflow is breaking down.

**为什么有效**：直接命中对方在招聘的岗位 = 你懂他现在的痛点。

### 2. 数据引用（适合所有 ToB）
> Your competitor [name] just launched [X], and their traffic jumped 30% in 2 weeks per SimilarWeb. Here is what's working for them and how you could do the same.

**为什么有效**：用第三方数据替代自我吹嘘，对方会自动脑补你也能做到。

### 3. 自我介绍（适合小众行业）
> I'm [name], I work with [N] [industry] brands on [problem], including [client name] (who 3x'd their [metric] last quarter).

**为什么有效**：给一个具体客户做 social proof，比说「业内领先」强 100 倍。

### 4. 推荐式（适合冷启动）
> [Mutual contact] suggested I reach out. We helped their team [specific result], and I think the same approach could work for you.

**为什么有效**：借熟人/同行推荐，对方心理防线降低 70%。

### 5. 资源先行（适合价值前置）
> I put together a teardown of [their product/website/marketing] — 3 quick wins you can ship this week. Sharing it in case it's useful.

**为什么有效**：先给价值，对方不好意思不回。即便不回，下次联系时印象分 +50。

## 反模式（千万别这么写）

- ❌ "Hope this email finds you well."
- ❌ "We are a leading manufacturer with 20 years experience..."
- ❌ "Our product is high quality and competitive..."

这些句子被 Gmail 自动归类为「广」邮件的概率是 90%。
